A research proof of concept

Get the app

Nearbytes is a proof of concept. In a browser it works at once, with nothing to install. The app on your device is faster, and keeps working with its window closed.

Android

Download the app's package and open it. Your phone asks whether to allow installing apps from your browser: allow it. It asks because this proof of concept is not in a store.

Mac

Open Terminal, paste this line and press Return:

curl -fsSL https://nearbytes-app.github.io/install.sh | sh

It downloads the app, checks its signature, and puts it in Applications in your home folder, with no administrator rights. Because the line downloads it, not a browser, macOS opens it without the warning for apps from outside the App Store; should it still refuse, open System Settings, Privacy & Security, and choose Open Anyway. For Macs with Apple silicon.

Windows

Open PowerShell, paste this line and press Enter:

irm https://nearbytes-app.github.io/install.ps1 | iex

It downloads the app, checks its signature, and puts it in your own folder, with a shortcut in the Start menu and no administrator rights.

Linux

Open a terminal, paste this line and press Enter:

curl -fsSL https://nearbytes-app.github.io/install.sh | sh

It downloads the app, checks its signature, and puts it in your own folder with an entry in your applications menu, with no administrator rights. It needs WebKitGTK, which most desktops have.

iPhone and iPad

There is no iPhone app to hand out yet: that needs Apple's developer programme. Use Nearbytes in Safari: it is the same app.

The app is not signed by Apple, Google or Microsoft, since a proof of concept has no store account. Each line above checks instead that what it downloads is signed with the project's own release key, and installs nothing otherwise.
